DivisiBill

DivisiBill is a smartphone program, a sucessor to an earlier product called BillSplitter. If you are tired of paying more than your fair share at a restaurant, DivisiBill is the answer.

DivisiBill lets you enter each item on a bill or scan the bill and get items entered automatically and indicate how each item should be shared among selected guests.

Then it calculates how much each guest owes, including tax and tip, or, if you enter the tax amount, it will tell you the corresponding tax rate. It can handle splitting discounts evenly, or unevenly, deal with tax calculations that are off by a few cents (surprisingly common) or a variety of other complex situations. The intent is to handle more or less any common scenario.

You can email the results to attendees, including an image of the bill if you wish, and DivisiBill can make a pretty good guess at the venue based on your location, if you've used it there before, automatically showing you the prior bill from that location.

DivisiBill is intended to handle complex bills where you want each person to pay their share. If all you want to do is split the bill evenly and maybe calculate a tip or tax, there are lots of free programs available to do that.

Privacy Policy

DivisiBill will not share your personal information at any time.

DivisiBill will connect to the Internet only at your request (for example in order to back up and restore files or send mail).
