Licensing

The Basic Edition of DivisiBill does not show ads and can handle most bills but it has some limitations. The settings page allows you to buy additional features. You can use this page to upgrade to the Professional Edition and once you have done that you can buy extra packages of OCR scans if you use up the included ones (for more details on OCR see below).

The Professional Edition also enables cloud backup and storage of bills, venue lists and people lists. The settings page for the professional Edition has a few additional options in order to support these features.

OCR

OCR stands for Optical Character recognition; it allows you to send a picture of the bill to a cloud based service to be scanned into item names and amounts and a few other useful values. It cannot help you distribute the cost of items - only you know who had what - but it can take a lot of the tedium out of the data entry process.

Each OCR scan costs a few cents, and once you purchase the Professional Edition you'll have an initial credit of 5 OCR scans. You can buy more in blocks of 30 at a time. You will retain any OCR credits through an upgrade or if you uninstall and reinstall DivisiBill.

Modern mobile phones can take very high resolution pictures, these are unnecessarily large and detailed for OCR so DivisiBill reduces the resolution and converts them to black and white before saving them to save storage space and to save transmission time when OCR is being performed.
