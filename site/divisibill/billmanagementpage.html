Bill List Management Page

This is where you go to manage the lists of bills (local and remote). At present it simply allows you to download all the bills that are available remotely but not locally.

There are several significant areas on this page.

Download

This button initiates a download of the files available remotely but not locally. Once pressed the UI will change to show the progress and/or allow the download to be canceled.

Flyout

Click on this icon to open the flyout and select other pages
