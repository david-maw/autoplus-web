People List

This is a list of the important areas on the People List page as seen from left to right, top to bottom. You can see an image of the page itself here. If you got here by tapping one of the active areas in the page image, the relevant heading should be highlighted below.

Icons

These appear in the toolbar at the top of the page or in the area revealed when you swipe on a line in the list.

Flyout ()

This icon, in the top left corner of the screen, allows you to show or collapse the flyout menu to navigate to other pages.

Download List (ﭙ)

Tap here to download a new list of people from the cloud.

Add ()

Opens a page to let you add a new person and specify their details.

Person

Each entry in this list represents a single person. You can swipe left to delete the entry, or swipe right to add the person to the current bill if you got to this page by requesting to add a person to the current bill (see here).

Tapping on a person will show details which you can edit.
