Venue List

This is a list of the important areas on the Venue List page as seen from left to right, top to bottom. You can see an image of the page itself here. If you reached this page by tapping one of the active areas in the page image, the relevant heading should be highlighted below.

Icons

These appear in the toolbar at the top of the page or in the area revealed when you swipe on a venue line.

Flyout ()

This icon, in the top left corner of the screen, allows you to show or collapse the flyout menu to navigate to other pages.

Download List (ﭙ)

Tap here to download a new list of venues from the cloud.

Sort ()

Sorts the list of venues alphabetically by name or by distance of the venue from your current location (venues with unknown locations sort last).

Add ()

Opens a page to let you add a new venue.

Heading

This shows the sort order currently in use.

Venue

Each entry in this list represents a single venue. You can swipe it left to delete the venue, or swipe right to make it the venue on the current bill (which may save the bill you were using previously if it has been changed).

Tapping on a venue will open a details page where you can edit the venue name or store notes about the venue. The location coordinates shown here are stored automatically when you assign the venue to a bill. Tapping on the coordinates will show the venue on a map.
