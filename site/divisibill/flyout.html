Flyout

When you click the hamburger icon () at the top left corner of the page, or pull out from the left margin, a "flyout" appears with a number of selections on it. It looks like this:

You can click on one of the items to close the flyout and select the corresponding page or function.

Click on a highlighted area above to see what it does. Also note:

  • "Items and Totals" gets you whichever of those 2 pages you last used. Switch between them by swiping left on Items, right on Totals.
  • "Help Index" shows you the index page for this set of help documents.
  • "Current Page Help" shows help for whatever page you most recently selected.
