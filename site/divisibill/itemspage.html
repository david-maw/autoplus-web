Items Page

This is where the individual items and the information about them is entered, either by scanning a paper bill or manually. It looks something like this (hover over the image for more information)

Comp

Sometimes items will be "comped" by a restaurant, usually by way of an apology because something went wrong. Clicking in this column allows you to toggle the Comped status of an item (comped items are free, but still contribute to the tip. If you don't want a comped item to contribute to the tip, just set the cost to zero, or delete the item altogether.

Icons

Select one of the icons on the top right of the page to send a copy of the bill by mail(󰄈), show items for all or only one person(/), add an additional item () or delete all items (啕).

Line Items

You can swipe individual items left to deletes the item (if you delete an item by mistake you can get it back until you exit the page). Tap on an item to make that the current one so you can edit the name or price and/or assign it to one or more people.

Open Flyout

This icon(), in the top left corner of the screen, allows you to show, or collapse, the flyout menu allowing navigation to other pages (navigate from the items page to the totals page by swiping left, back by swiping right).

Sharing Control

Tap here to remove the item assignments if there are any, if there are none, tapping will share the item cost in the same proportion as the rest of the bill so far. You can repeat this once the whole bill has been allocated if you want to make it more representative, alternatively, you can designate how the amount is shared manually just as for any other amount. For comped items only the tip is shared since the other amounts (cost and tax) are zero.

Venue Name

This is the name of the current venue, tap on it to see the properties (tax and tip rates, for example) of the current bill, double tap to select a new venue from your list.
