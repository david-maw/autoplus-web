DivisiBill

DivisiBill is simple in concept, there are two lists, participants and items. Each item has a description and a cost which is divided (perhaps unevenly) between one or more participants. The program does the rest, calculating tax and tip and dividing them fairly among the participants.

Each item on a bill can be entered manually or you can scan the bill and get items entered automatically but either way you get to enter the list of participants and the division of each item between them.

Pages

There are over a dozen different pages in Divisibill but the most important are Items and Totals, where you list as many purchased items as you like and Totals where the total amount for each of up to 10 participants is listed.

Other pages show a list of all possible people you might use on bills along with optional e-mail addresses, a list of possible venues with optional notes and locations, properties of the current bill, program settings and so on the main pages are:

  • Items and Totals
  • Bill Image
  • Bill Properties
  • List of Saved Bills sorted by Age, Name or Distance
  • List of venues sorted by Name or Distance
  • List of People
  • Program Settings
  • Help

There are a few other subsidiary pages reached from these, but these are the main ones.
