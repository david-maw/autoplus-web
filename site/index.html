DivisiBill by AutoPlus

If you are tired of unfair results at a restaurant when the bill is split evenly, DivisiBill is the answer.

DivisiBill lets you take a picture of the bill and get items entered automatically, enter each item on a bill manually, or a combination of the two. Once entered you get to indicate who pays for each item. For items that are shared you can indicate who shared them, and, if necessary, share the cost unevenly.

With that information it calculates how much each guest owes, including tax and tip, or, if you enter the tax amount, it will tell you the corresponding tax rate. It can handle splitting discounts evenly, or unevenly, deal with tax calculations that are off by a few cents (surprisingly common) or a variety of other complex situations. The intent is to handle more or less any common scenario.

You can email the results to attendees, including an image of the bill if you wish, and DivisiBill can make a pretty good guess at the venue based on a GPS location, if you've used it there before, automatically showing you the prior bill from that location.

DivisiBill is intended to handle complex bills where you want each person to pay their share. If all you want to do is split the bill evenly and maybe calculate a tip or tax, there are lots of free programs available to do that.
